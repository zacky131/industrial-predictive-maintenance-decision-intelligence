# Industrial Predictive Maintenance & Decision Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project explores how predictive analytics could support economically justified maintenance decisions for a medium-sized Indonesian manufacturer. It begins by defining the management decision, measurable business outcomes, and constraints before selecting data or analytical methods. The intended outcome is a decision framework that helps maintenance teams act early where intervention is likely to reduce operational and financial losses.

## Business Problem

Unplanned machine failures disrupt production, trigger emergency maintenance, and delay customer orders. Limited visibility into machine health also makes it difficult to prioritize maintenance resources across approximately 100 machines. Management needs a practical way to focus inspections and interventions where they can have the greatest operational and financial impact.
